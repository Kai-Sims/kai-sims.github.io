01 About

Concept to hardware, end to end.

I am a mechanical engineer who earned my undergraduate degree from UC San Diego and am currently pursuing a Master of Engineering at UC Berkeley.

My experience spans precision mechanical design, manufacturing systems, automation, medical devices, prototyping, and structural design. I have worked on projects ranging from an autonomous manufacturing process for nano-scale 3D printer ink cartridges, to a robotic-compatible endoscopic clip applier, to racecar suspension and structural systems developed through CAD, FEA, fabrication, and testing.

What I’m looking for

I am interested in mechanical design, manufacturing, test, and hardware engineering roles where I can work on real systems from concept through prototyping, validation, and iteration. I am particularly interested in fast-paced teams where I can take ownership, keep learning, and grow quickly as an engineer.
