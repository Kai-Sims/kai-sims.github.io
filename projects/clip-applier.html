Endoscopic Clip Applier

Design Lead | Berkeley, CA | 2024 – 2025

I led the design and development of an endoscopic clip applier intended to interface with the Da Vinci surgical robot. The project required precise mechanical actuation, constrained packaging, and repeated prototype iteration to achieve reliable clip deployment.

What I worked on

  • Led a team of four undergraduate engineers through concept development, CAD design, prototyping, and testing.
  • Designed custom machined parts and mechanical interfaces for the clip deployment mechanism.
  • Managed tolerance stack-ups and mechanical alignment requirements for reliable actuation.
  • Coordinated prototype builds and used testing feedback to refine the design.

Key engineering challenges

  • Packaging a functional mechanism within a constrained robotic surgical form factor.
  • Maintaining consistent actuation and alignment across iterations.
  • Balancing manufacturability with precision mechanical performance.

What I learned

  • How to lead a small engineering team through an end-to-end hardware project.
  • How to refine mechanisms through rapid prototyping and structured testing.
  • How precision interfaces and tolerance decisions directly affect system reliability.
