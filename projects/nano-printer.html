Autonomous Nano-Scale 3D Printer Manufacturing Line

Design Leader | Berkeley, CA | August 2025 – Present

I am currently developing an autonomous manufacturing process for filling ink cartridges used in a nano-scale 3D printer. This project combines precision mechanical design, fluid handling, sensing, motion control, and Python-based automation to create a repeatable, production-oriented process.

What I worked on

  • Designed precision mechanical fixtures and fluid handling assemblies for automated cartridge filling.
  • Integrated sensors and stepper-driven motion hardware to coordinate positioning and process flow.
  • Developed Python-based automation for sequencing, data acquisition, and process control.
  • Managed tolerance stack-ups and alignment constraints to maintain micron-level consistency.

Key engineering challenges

  • Maintaining repeatable alignment across multiple manufacturing cycles.
  • Coordinating hardware, sensors, and control logic into a reliable automated workflow.
  • Identifying and reducing sources of variability through testing and root cause analysis.

What I learned

  • How small tolerance and alignment changes can strongly affect process performance.
  • How to design automation around real manufacturing constraints rather than ideal assumptions.
  • How to integrate mechanical systems and software into a cohesive production process.
